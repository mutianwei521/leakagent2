# L-TOWN network model: attribution

`L-TOWN.inp` (v1.2) was developed by the KIOS Research and Innovation Center of
Excellence, University of Cyprus, for the Battle of the Leakage Detection and
Isolation Methods (BattLeDIM 2020). It is redistributed here unmodified, for
research reproducibility, under the competition's terms.

- Competition: https://battledim.ucy.ac.cy/
- Dataset and rules: Vrachimis et al., BattLeDIM 2020 (dataset description and
  competition rules, v1.3.1).

The competition SCADA time series and leak labels used by the L-Town leg are
NOT redistributed here; download them from the competition site and point
`data/ltown_setup.py` / `eval/run_ltown.py` at your local copy. L-Town is a
simulated benchmark generated by the organisers; it is not measured utility
telemetry.

Every other file in this folder (partitions, adjacency, sensor fingerprints,
topology index) is derived by this repository's scripts.
