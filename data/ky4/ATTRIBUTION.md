# KY4 network model: attribution

`ky4.inp` originates from the Kentucky water distribution system research
database (University of Kentucky, UKnowledge) and is vendored here unmodified
from the model library bundled with the WNTR package (`wntr/library/networks`),
for research reproducibility.

- Database: Jolly, M. D., Lothes, A. D., Bryson, L. S. & Ormsbee, L.
  "Research database of water distribution system models."
  J. Water Resour. Plann. Manage. 140, 410-416 (2014).
- WNTR: Klise, K. A. et al., Sandia National Laboratories / U.S. EPA.

Every other file in this folder (partitions, adjacency, sensor fingerprints,
topology index, and the rebuildable leak cache) is derived by this repository's
scripts.
